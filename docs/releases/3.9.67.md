# AutoFish 3.9.67 — Trophy Capture Fix, Lightbox Preview & Dark Scrollbars

## Summary

- **White-flash trophy capture fix** (`autofish.js`, `src/timing.js`, `src/loop.js`):
  - Holds dismissal on Legendary, Secret, Shiny, and Golden catches (`'trophy-wait'`, bounded to `900 ms` via `trophySettleMs`) until `isCardSettled()` confirms dark artwork/text ink instead of snapping Pixi's initial white pop-in silhouette.
  - Expands the capture rect with `padCardBox()` (`+25%` horizontal, `+32%` top, `+12%` bottom, clamped to canvas bounds) so overflowing rare artwork is preserved, raises max width to `480px`, and encodes as transparent `image/webp` (`image/png` fallback) instead of opaque JPEG black bars.
- **IndexedDB Chromium transaction fix** (`panel.js`): Runs `store.delete()` synchronously inside `IDBRequest.onsuccess` and attaches transaction completion listeners before awaiting, fixing silent `TransactionInactiveError` failures in `listTrophyCards()` and `clearTrophyCards()`.
- **Compact trophy rows + centered Lightbox preview** (`panel.js`):
  - Replaces tall stacked cards with scannable horizontal `<button class="trophy-item">` rows showing a framed `46×52px` thumbnail, species name, color-coded rarity/variant pills (`TIER_COLORS`), weight, value, and time.
  - Clicking any row opens a centered full-viewport Lightbox dialog displaying the full-res card over a dim blurred backdrop with `← Prev` / `Next →` (`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`), `Save image`, and `Escape` / backdrop close.
- **Modern dark scrollbars & viewport safety** (`panel.js`, `dashboard.html`): Styles dark translucent scrollbars across the panel Shadow DOM and Firefox/Chromium dashboard, and caps `.body` height (`calc(100vh - 70px)`) with smooth scrolling on compact screens.
