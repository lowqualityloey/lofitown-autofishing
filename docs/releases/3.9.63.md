## What changed

3.9.63 is a diagnostics release. It does not change fishing behavior, the planner or the press gate.

### Guard and blind state travel with the export

`__fish.exportRun()` now includes `guard`, `blind` and `diagError` when the panel is mounted. The panel builds one shared `blindFacts()` snapshot for both the warning banner and the export, and exports the raw silence age even below the three-minute warning threshold, so a five-minute sampler cannot miss a warning that appeared and cleared between posts.

The guard snapshot carries `revives`, `windowRevives`, `tickErrors`, `lastTickError`, `doctor` and `tickAgoMs`. A missing or throwing panel hook records `diagError` and leaves the export intact; the night's record still posts.

### The morning report explains guard and blind state

The overnight report now reads the optional `note` column on `sessions.csv` and prints a `## Session notes` section. It also aggregates the maximum detector silence, the number of snapshots with a blind warning, and peak guard revives under `## Guard and blind`. Snapshots from older builds print `_not recorded in these snapshots (pre-3.9.63)._` rather than implying a quiet night.
