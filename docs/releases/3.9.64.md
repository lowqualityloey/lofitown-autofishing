## What changed

3.9.64 fixes the mid-fish dial lock drops, 12-press stall cap, 1-frame re-arm lag, and off-center seat prompt blindness observed in live BrowserOS Neo sessions.

### Dial lock continuity and 1-frame re-arm

- `doPress()` now clears `F.geo._mid`, `_mt`, `_om`, and `_fit` alongside `_hist` so `F.sample()` tracks a newly spawned triangle at any angle instead of rejecting >90° spawns for 4 frames, stalling 450 ms in `relockGraceMs`, dropping `F.geo`, and restarting `pressesThisDial = 0` (`firstPressHeld`).
- `spawnProven` / `rearmReady()` now runs **before** `planPress()` inside `step()`, allowing an accepted spawn to arm and schedule/press on the same frame.
- `maxPressPerDial` is raised from `12` to `30`, paired with a consecutive-rejected guard (`maxRejectedPerDial: 5`), so high-tier fish requiring 13+ accepted presses do not stall until the 15 s escape timer.

### Off-center pier seat prompts and WebGL snapshot recovery

- `seatPromptBox(F.lastGeo, cw, ch)` adds a clamped 260×150 prompt scan box centered above the locked dial (`cx, cy - 53`) to `bubbleGate()` and `bubbleInBox()`, giving off-center pier seats (`y ≈ 580`) native `via: 'box'` detection and `glyphMetrics` protection.
- Locking any dial now updates `F.hint = { x: g.cx, y: g.cy }`, preventing `dialAnywhere()` 2.5 s fallbacks on every dial at off-center seats.
- `bubbleAnywhere()` attaches `glyphMetrics`, and `step()` presses the game's fishing keybind on rung 1 when an `anywhere` sighting is `glyph === true` (without tapping `anywhere` blobs).
- `downscaleGl()` retries WebGL recovery up to 3 failures before falling back permanently to 2D `getImageData`.
