# AutoFish 3.9.69 — Authentic Catch Card Lightbox & PNG Export

## Summary

- **Eliminated White-Card `uiCanvas` Captures** (`autofish.js`):
  - Removed `captureCardBlob()` and the `900 ms` `trophy-wait` delay so trophy recording never pauses fishing or stores blank white `uiCanvas` silhouettes.
  - Wired `F.onTrophy(row, null)` immediately and persisted `quality` (`Exquisite`, `Fine`, `Decent`, etc.) in `saveTrophyCard()` (`panel.js`).
- **Authentic lofi.town Catch Card Lightbox + PNG Export** (`panel.js`):
  - Loads official UI badges (`Fish-Tier-Rare`, `Fish-Tier-Epic`, `Fish-Tier-Legendary`, `Fish-Tier-Secret`, `shiny-star/star4`) from `sheet-ui-fishing-0.json` alongside the 4 fish sprite atlases.
  - Clicking any trophy row (including previously saved `v3.9.68` records) renders a rarity-themed lofi.town catch card with the crisp pixel-art fish sprite, nameplate, tier badge, `weight`, `MM/DD/YY` date, `quality`, and `$value`.
  - **Save image** renders that card onto a `320×390` canvas and exports `autofish-<species>.png`.
