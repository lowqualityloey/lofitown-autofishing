# AutoFish 3.9.68 — Sprite-Atlas Trophy Icons, Epic Capture & Banner Lifecycle Fixes

## Summary

- **Official Game Sprites in Trophy List + Full-Card Lightbox** (`panel.js`):
  - Lazily loads lofi.town's four fish texture-pack atlases (`sheet-fish-standard-0.json`, `sheet-fish-swamp-0.json`, `sheet-fish-arctic-0.json`, `sheet-fish-volcanic-0.json`) on opening the Trophy viewer and renders crisp `image-rendering: pixelated` **Base** and **Shiny** fish sprites (plus Golden framing glow) in each list row, falling back to `rec.blob` if offline.
  - Clicking any trophy row still opens your **actual captured catch card** in the centered Lightbox (with the pixel-art sprite as a clean fallback when no blob exists).
- **Epic Catches Included (`EPI`) with Priority Eviction** (`autofish.js`, `panel.js`):
  - Adds **Epic** catches to the 24h Trophy viewer (`EPI` filter tab and clickable `EPI` rarity strip trigger) and raises `TROPHY_CAP` to `40`.
  - Keeps `Rare catch chime` scoped strictly to `Legendary`, `Secret`, `Shiny`, and `Golden` (`isRareChimeRow`), and evicts plain `Epic` cards first so Legendary/Secret/Shiny/Golden catches are never overwritten by Epics.
- **Wider Rare-Art Capture Headroom** (`src/timing.js`):
  - Expands `padCardBox()` padding (`+30%` horizontal, `+36%` top, `+12%` bottom) so large overflowing art keeps intact tentacles/fins.
- **Stale Banner Lifecycle Fixes** (`panel.js`):
  - Clears leftover reload/info banners cleanly on accepted **Start**, **Resume**, **Start now**, and user **Stop** (`clearBannerState()` in `dispatch()`), auto-expires `"session resumed after the reload"` after 8 seconds as a green notice, and clears `"session log offline — the session still works"` when a subsequent `/session` POST succeeds.
