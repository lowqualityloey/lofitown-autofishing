# AutoFish 3.9.75 — Trophy Retention, Cleanup & List Triage

## Summary

- **Retention boundary and Keep** (`panel.js`):
  - The trophy viewer now shows the 40-card cap, saved/missing counts, kept cards, remaining slots, the nearest expiry and the next likely eviction before a catch can remove a card.
  - `Keep` pins a card past the 24-hour TTL without bypassing the hard cap; eviction still prefers unprotected Epics over Legendary, Secret, Shiny, and Golden cards. `Keep` is reversible.
- **Reversible saved-card cleanup** (`panel.js`):
  - A two-step `clean` action runs the same detached-sparkle cleanup used for new captures over saved card PNGs, stores the original as a backup, reports progress and skips, and leaves a failed card's blob untouched.
  - `restore` returns the original image and removes the backup.
- **Filter counts and empty context** (`panel.js`):
  - Tab counts refresh after render, save, prune, and clear; each filter has a named empty state.
  - The Event tab explains that capture starts with this build and can show the all-time ledger counts while the viewer is otherwise empty.
- **Species search, sort, and grouping** (`panel.js`):
  - Adds species search plus newest, rarity, and weight sorting, and groups duplicate cards behind a `×N` control while keeping every card reachable in the lightbox.
  - Groups sort by their visible representative, so a heavier card hidden inside a group cannot jump the group ahead of what is shown. Toggling `Keep` keeps the open group and position.
- **Capture and card resilience** (`autofish.js`, `panel.js`, `src/timing.js`):
  - Detached sparkle components are stripped from capture PNGs; event-rarity cards are saved, and missing screenshots now carry the reason they are missing.
  - A detached or superseded image load can no longer replace the current card with a stale error, an unreadable blob shows the card placeholder, and legacy missing cards explain that they predate failure reasons.
- **HUD polish** (`panel.js`):
  - Body rows no longer flex-shrink, tier padding no longer shifts the `EPI` / `LEG` / `SEC` counts, and the capture line no longer clips its last field.
- **Compatibility**: no fishing loop, dial timing, seat handling, or session behavior changed.
