## What changed

3.9.51 makes each overnight session row name its profile, so the morning report can bound a named night instead of borrowing another profile's hours. It also improves the first press on a short-lived dial: the bot re-arms when the dial is proven, and it skips a first-press hold that cannot mature.

### Session rows carry their profile

- `sessions.csv` now ends with a `profile` column; the panel posts its profile with the row, and fishserve stores it.
- Existing five-column rows still read as `default`. The header is upgraded in place; no row is rewritten or backfilled.
- `POST /session` reduces `profile` and `reason` to short token strings, so a hand-written request cannot add a column or inject a row.
- `GET /sessions?day=&profile=` returns only the requested profile's rows and totals.
- The morning report filters rows to the profiles its snapshots name. A named profile is bounded by its own row, never another profile's session.

### A faster, better-judged first press

- The bot re-arms when a new triangle is proven or a recent press was accepted, instead of also waiting for `|delta| > win + 12`. A wedge already under the pin is pressed at once; `pressGapMs` still spaces presses.
- A first-press hold is skipped when its remaining budget is shorter than the 150 ms fit, rather than holding a press that cannot mature.
- The corrected first-press gate treats `firstPressHeld` as rare, requires `holdPassesMax` under one rotation, and compares `firstPressForced` with 3.9.49's 37 - not with held.
