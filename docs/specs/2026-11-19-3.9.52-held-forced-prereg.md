## Metrics

Collect one full sample at the end of each session from `status()`/`report()`:

| Metric | Definition | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `D` | `stats.dials` | Denominator |
| `H` | `stats.firstPressHeld` | Held first presses |
| `F` | `stats.firstPressForced` | All forced first presses |
| `HF` | `stats.firstPressHeldForced` | Held then forced |
| `HNF` | `H - HF` | Held but not forced; proxy only, not confirmed matured |
| `heldRate` | `H / D` | Existing 20% cap input |
| `heldForcedRate` | `HF / D` | Primary held-forced metric |
| `heldForcedShare` | `HF / H` | Secondary metric |
| `catchRate` | `caught / (caught + lost)` | Safety floor |

Record `caught`, `lost`, `misses`, `pressRejected`, and `holdPassesMax` with every sample. Do not call `HNF` matured unless an explicit maturity outcome exists.

## Sample-size validation

At `H = 60` and a share near 0.50, the normal-approximation 95% half-width is:

```text
1.96 * sqrt(0.25 / 60) = 0.1265
```

That is about **12.6 percentage points**. A 30/60 observation has a one-sided 95% Wilson upper bound of about **0.604**, so it cannot establish that the share is at most 0.50. A 20/60 observation has an upper bound of about **0.439**, but 60 held dials is a narrow, conditional case, not a sufficient minimum for retirement decisions.

For a roughly ±10-point 95% planning interval at a 0.50 share:

```text
1.96^2 * 0.25 / 0.10^2 = 96.04
```

The minimum is therefore **96 held dials**. This is a planning target, not a claim that an observed 0.50 share becomes conclusive; the confidence-bound gate below handles that.

## Minimum sample

All of these are required before any retirement decision:

- **Three clean sessions**, each with at least **200 dials**.
- **At least 600 dials total** (`D >= 600`).
- **At least 96 held dials** (`H >= 96`).
- **At least 24 held-forced events** (`HF >= 24`).
- The aggregate sample must satisfy the existing cap: **`H / D <= 0.20`**. A cap breach disqualifies retirement.
- No code or behavior changes during sampling; no mid-session reloads or hold-policy tuning.

At the observed 3.9.51 held rate of roughly 10.5–17.35%, 600 dials yields about 63–104 held dials. If `H < 96` or `HF < 24`, extend the sample; do not lower either minimum.

## Confidence-bound rule

The held-forced share is judged with a **one-sided 95% Wilson score upper bound** for `HF / H`, not by its point estimate alone. The retirement gate is:

```text
upper95(HF, H) <= 0.50
```

The bound must clear 0.50 before the cap can be retired. For scale at the 96-held minimum, 38/96 has an upper bound of about 0.480, while 40/96 is about 0.501; a result near 0.50 will not clear the gate.

If the upper bound does not clear 0.50, keep the cap and extend the sample. Do not replace the bound with a point estimate.

## Retirement criteria

The 20% held-rate cap is retired only when **all** are true:

1. The minimum sample above is complete.
2. `HF / D <= 0.10` — at most one in ten dials pays a held-then-forced hold.
3. `HF / H <= 0.50` — at most half of held dials are forced.
4. The one-sided 95% Wilson upper bound for `HF / H` is `<= 0.50`.
5. `catchRate >= 0.96` — close to the 3.9.51 baseline of 58/60 = **96.7%**, no more than 0.7 points below it.
6. Zero press rejections and zero misses in the qualifying sample.

If any criterion fails, the 20% cap stays. Do not evaluate retirement early, cherry-pick sessions, or tune the hold policy during observation. Retirement means replacing the 20% cap with the direct held-forced gates; telemetry remains.
